About TechGates

TechGates is a computer store in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. We sell gaming PCs, laptops, and parts, and we help you understand what you are buying before you pay for it.

Buying hardware usually means comparing long spec lists. TechGates puts a plain-language hardware guide next to the shop, so you can learn what each part does and choose what fits the way you use your computer.
