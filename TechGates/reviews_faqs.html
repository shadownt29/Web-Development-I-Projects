Frequently asked questions

Common questions answered before you make a purchase.

Are all hardware components authentic and backed by warranty?

Yes. All laptops, ready-built PCs, and standalone PC parts are 100% genuine and sourced through official regional distributors. Standard manufacturer warranties range from 1 to 3 years.

How does the custom builder guide help ensure compatibility?

The builder guide outlines CPU socket requirements, memory generations (DDR4 vs DDR5), and power supply wattage guidelines so you do not buy mismatched components.

What are the shipping times and coverage areas?

Deliveries within Phnom Penh typically take 24 to 48 hours. We also ship across all Cambodian provinces via reliable express couriers with tracking provided upon dispatch.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Bakong / KHQR transfers across all major Cambodian banks, plus cash on delivery for orders delivered within central Phnom Penh.
